Understanding the Metrics:
  • Next-pack score — Expected number of Pokémon species you don't own yet from one pack. It counts the common, uncommon and reverse-holo slots: 4 commons, 3 uncommons and 2 reverse holos for Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution, 5 commons, 3 uncommons and 1 reverse holo for Sword & Shield. Every card in a slot is assumed equally likely. A species with more cards is more likely to show up, but pulling it twice counts once. Trainers and Special Energy take up slots but are never useful. The rare slot and the hits (ex, V, Illustration Rare and higher) are not included. Higher = more new Pokémon expected from the next pack. Sets with another pack format or without common/uncommon totals are marked Fallback and are ranked after the others by Pull Chance %.
  • Pull Chance % — Likelihood that a pulled Pokémon will be NEW. Accounts for rarity (Common = easier to pull). Higher = better chance of getting something new!
  • Missing % — Simple percentage of unique Pokémon you still need (ignores rarity). Higher = more of the set is useful.
  • Abs. Score — Total weighted value of missing Pokémon. Larger sets score higher. Use this to compare raw potential.
Tip: Use "Best Next Pack" to decide which booster pack to buy next. Ties are broken by Pull Chance %.
